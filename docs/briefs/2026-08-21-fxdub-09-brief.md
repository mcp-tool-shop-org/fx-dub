## The third door: `LoadVideo` → `GetVideoComponents`

Core ships **`GetVideoComponents`** (category `video`): it takes `VIDEO` and outputs **`IMAGE`, `AUDIO`, `FLOAT` (framerate), `INT` (bit depth)**. That is exactly the conversion the graph needs — `LoadVideo` reads the asset namespace, and `GetVideoComponents` turns it into the IMAGE batch that `VHS_SelectEveryNthImage`, `Florence2Run`, and `VHS_VideoCombine` already consume. No VHS input folder involved at all.

This is also the better long-term architecture: a published fx-dub will take *users'* footage, which arrives through the attachment path, not through a pre-seeded input folder.
