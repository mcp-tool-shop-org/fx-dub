## 6. Two new platform findings you may want

**6.1 `SaveVideo.codec.encoding` crashes local pre-flight.** Sending `"codec.encoding": "auto"`
returns `candidate.toLowerCase is not a function` and no verdict. Its options are **objects**
(`{"key":"auto","inputs":{…}}`), not strings, and the validator lowercases them. Omitting the field
validates clean. Second-level dotted fields whose options are objects are the hazard.

**6.2 `ImageFromBatch` clamps silently, and `GetVideoComponents` decodes at the source rate.**
`GetVideoComponents` on the 473-frame / 47 fps output returned **161 frames at 16 fps**. An out-of-
range `batch_index` does not error — it returns the **last** frame. We only caught it because two
requested indices came back with identical content hashes. **Anyone reading a specific frame from a
batch can silently get a different frame than they asked for**, which for our purpose (reading face
coordinates off a named frame) would have been a wrong answer with no error.

## 7. Two more broken things, found after the audition

Both would cost you a round if you hit them cold.

**7.1 `AudioVideoCombine` is broken on Comfy Cloud.** It is the obvious node for laying
an audio track over a VIDEO — `audio` in, `video` in, VIDEO out — and every call raises:

```
ImportError: TorchCodec is required for save_with_torchcodec.
```

Job `3397f3e9-f7bd-44f1-8b37-6b585fa5599a`. It joins `AudioPad` in our broken-on-cloud
registry. **The working mux is `GetVideoComponents → VHS_VideoCombine(images, audio)`**,
with `frame_rate` driven by the LINK from `GetVideoComponents` slot 2 — never a bare
widget, which reads 8 underneath and silently doubles the duration.

**7.2 `AudioMix`'s gains are clamped to −24…+6 dB.** Submitting `gain_2_db: 7.0` returns
`value_bigger_than_max`. Our own delivered-mix recipe recorded "VO +7 dB, bed
`gain_1_db` −12" — which **cannot have come from `AudioMix` at all**. The VO boost is an
`AudioAdjustVolume` node ahead of the bus. Rebuilt that way the mix came back
**byte-identical** to the delivered one by FLAC STREAMINFO md5 (`c34976a8…`).

A recipe that names a gain without naming the node that applies it is not reproducible.
That is our defect, and it sat in our handoff for a day.
